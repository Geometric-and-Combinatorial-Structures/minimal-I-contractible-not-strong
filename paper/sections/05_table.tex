\section{The extended cardinality table}\label{sec:table}

Table~\ref{tab:card} lists, for connected graphs on $n\le 12$ vertices, the number of graphs in each of the classes of Section~\ref{sec:prelim}. The rows $n\le 11$ coincide cell by cell with Table~1 of~\cite{DEFH2023}; the column $\I$ is new (it was obtained by certifying every $\Ztwo$-acyclic graph outside $\IS$ with Algorithm~\ref{alg:icert}, which for $n\le 12$ is vacuous since every $\Ztwo$-acyclic graph is in $\IS$). The row $n=12$ was computed on the cluster in $8$ minutes (Table~\ref{tab:runs}) and gives the new value $|\IVS(12)|=\num{22666525713}$; the columns $3$-Dis, $2$-Dis, $1$-Dis and $(-1)$-Dis for $n=12$ were not part of that run and are being computed together with the row $n=13$.

Several patterns in the table are worth recording.

\begin{observation}\label{obs:table}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item For $n\le 12$ every $\Ztwo$-acyclic graph is strong I-contractible: $\Ztwo\text{-acyclic}=\I=\IS$. The first exceptions occur at $n=13$ and are the three torsion graphs of Theorem~\ref{thm:A}; in particular ``$\Ztwo$-acyclic'' and ``$\ZZ$-acyclic'' coincide for $n\le 12$. The first $\ZZ$-acyclic graphs outside $\IS$, and the first members of $\I\setminus\IS$, occur at $n=14$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:B}).
\item $\IVS=2\text{-Dis}=3\text{-Dis}$ for all $n\le 11$, and $|\IVS(12)|$ agrees with the value of $|2\text{-Dis}(12)|$ obtained in an independent (unpublished, 2025) computation by the authors; thus $\IVS=2\text{-Dis}$ up to $12$ vertices, and a graph in $\IVS$ that is not $2$-dismantlable, if any, has at least $13$ vertices.
\item $\IS\ne\IVS$ first at $n=11$ (twelve graphs; the smallest members of $\IS\setminus\IVS$ of~\cite{DEFH2023}), and $|\IS(12)|-|\IVS(12)|=\num{11718}$.
\item $\chi=1$ but not $\Ztwo$-acyclic first at $n=7$ (one graph), and the gap grows to $\num{9816037137}$ graphs at $n=12$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{observation}
