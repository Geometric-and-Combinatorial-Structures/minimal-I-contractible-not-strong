# Accompanying article

**Jesús F. Espinoza, Martín Eduardo Frías-Armenta, Rosalía G. Hernández-Amador and Héctor A. Hernández-Hernández.** *Counterexamples and enumeration for contractible and dismantlable graphs* (2026), manuscript in preparation.

[Read the PDF](paper/main.pdf) · [Browse the LaTeX sources](paper/) · [Citation metadata](CITATION.cff)

The present source and PDF correspond to the complete arXiv-format draft r01 prepared on 10 November 2026. This identifies a manuscript version, not an arXiv submission or a published article. The current repository adds the computational companion and a separately numbered [mathematical supplement](supplement/main.pdf).

## Mathematical contribution

The article develops one 13-vertex witness and one 14-vertex witness in full, treats the variants compactly, and proves the algorithms underlying the cardinality table. The 13-vertex complex is mod-2 acyclic with first integral homology of order three. The 14-vertex graph is I-contractible and not strong I-contractible, but an admissible edge addition makes it strong. These constructions separate the two assertions of Conjecture 14 and refute Conjecture 15 of Dochtermann et al. (2023).

Rows 3–13 classify all connected graphs; the entire row 14 classifies connected K4-free graphs. The minimum order 14 is global. The eight classes and the minimum of 44 edges concern the K4-free order-14 universe. Equality of integral acyclicity and I-contractibility is asserted only within the classified universes.

The supplement develops the finite admissible-edge closures, the natural unfoldings of the 51 stuck seeds, the 20 strictly 2-dismantlable graphs of order 12, and the comparison with the earlier 15-vertex example. Computational commands live in the repository documentation; the mathematical arguments are in the article and supplement.

## Build

With a TeX installation containing `latexmk`, `amsart`, Palatino, Euler and TikZ:

```bash
python3 tools/build_paper.py --output build/paper
python3 tools/build_paper.py --source supplement --output build/supplement
```

Each build requires a new output directory, compiles a temporary source copy, and writes its PDF, logs and source-hash record there. The deposited `main.bbl` is used unchanged by default and its hash is recorded. Maintainers can explicitly request `--refresh-bibliography` to run BibTeX in the temporary copy; that does not change the distributed sources. The distributed PDFs are kept for convenient reading. A PDF regenerated by another TeX installation may have different bytes; the sources and mathematical contents determine the version.

The deposited bibliography contains the cited literature and DOI or publisher links where available. There are no third-party literature PDFs in this repository. See [bibliography notes](docs/bibliography.md) for the relation between the main references and the results used here.
